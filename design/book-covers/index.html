Proposed house style

Fontes / Covenant Forms

  1. A story, distilled. Two or three meaningful symbols per cover, selected from the book’s themes.
  2. Flat, tactile shapes. Solid fills, deliberate negative space. No gradients, shadows, stock imagery or embedded raster art.
  3. A family resemblance. Consistent arch, horizon, proportions and palette. Distinct compositions for each book.
  4. Quiet beside Scripture. Illustration belongs on the opening page; reading remains clear and uncluttered.
