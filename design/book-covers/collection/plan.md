# Covenant Forms — 66-book composition plan

Approved house style; individual compositions are editorial proposals. All 66 first-edition SVG covers are now drawn; individual art and source boundaries remain reviewable. Introduction source IDs are locators, not verified extraction boundaries.

## Visual rules

Flat symbolic landscapes; recurring arch and horizon; cream, ink, rust, gold, sage and water palette. Two or three primary motifs. No embedded raster art. No human depiction of God. Preserve room for book typography and the attributed introduction.
